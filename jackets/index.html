Which jacket suits a Lahore winter?

Lahore's cold is a December and January cold: dry, foggy mornings and evenings that drop fast after sunset. A bomber's ribbed collar, cuffs and hem are built for exactly that, sealing warmth in without the bulk of a long coat. The faux-leather styles (Onyx and Midnight Panel) shrug off fog and drizzle best. The jacquard styles (Copper Python and Navy Python) are the dressed-up option, with the pattern woven into the fabric. The Monochrome Block track jacket is the lightest of the seven, made for layering over a tee or under a heavier coat on the coldest days.
