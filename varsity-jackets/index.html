How to wear a varsity jacket in Lahore

Varsity jackets do the styling for you, so keep everything else plain: a white or black tee, straight denim and clean trainers is the whole formula. On colder January evenings, layer a plain hoodie underneath and leave the jacket open. Because both of our varsity styles are black and white, they also sit well over shalwar kameez for a Friday look that stays comfortable in and out of the mosque and lunch after. Avoid busy prints underneath: the chest panel and striped trims are the detail, and they read best against solid colours.
